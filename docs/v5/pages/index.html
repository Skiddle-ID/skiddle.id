v5 · Logbook

A redesign of every page. Each one is a page of your logbook: posts, builds, plays and flights are all entries, because since v4 they're all records in your repo. It keeps the terminal, adds the flight deck, and shows the protocol underneath.

Mono speaks for the machine; Sans speaks for Jeff. Labels, titles, dates and code are Geist Mono. Anything you'd read as a sentence is Geist Sans.

The repo · protocol

v4 put everything in AT Protocol. v5 lets it show, quietly.

Fast and accessible, by design

Budget per page, gzipped
WhatBudgetHow
CSS≤ 10 KBone stylesheet, no framework
JavaScript≤ 3 KBclock, theme, search, slides, checklist; all optional
Fonts2 × ~12 KBMono 400 and Sans 400 preloaded, the rest on use
Third parties0everything from the site's own domain
Layout shift0every image sized, no injected banners
Largest painttextthe heading, never waiting on an image
  • WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text in both themes and every accent, 3:1 for input borders, visible focus everywhere.
  • Works without JavaScript: menus are links, sliders scroll, search falls back to the writing page.
  • Nothing moves for people who ask for less motion; the testimonials have a Pause button.
  • Colour is never the only signal: every type, state and part has a word too.
  • Targets meet the 24 px minimum of WCAG 2.2; buttons are 36–44 px.
  • Windows high contrast keeps every border, waypoint and badge. It has its own CSS rules, but isn't tested on Windows yet.
  • Indonesian titles carry lang="id", so screen readers pronounce them right.
  • Page changes cross-fade with view transitions, no script.

For you to decide

  1. Logbook as the v5 direction?It replaces the open choice between home designs A–D. Bits of A (terminal) and C (tmux) live on in it.
  2. The menu: writing · work · flying · about · usesWorks, projects and services become "work", as the roadmap planned. Now, listening and notes sit under about and in the footer.
  3. The default accentBlue stays the default. Magenta, the colour of a flight display's active leg, is new; try it in the yellow panel.
  4. How much flyingStrips, routes, Zulu time and the hold-short line are subtle on their own. Each can go without breaking the rest.
