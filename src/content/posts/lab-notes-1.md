## Welcome to Lab Notes

Skiddle Labs is my company, and **Lab Notes** is where I'll write about what's going on there: one or two posts every week.

[Rebuilding skiddle.id](/series/#rebuilding-skiddle-id) ended with [part 7](/posts/never-quite-done/), so Lab Notes picks up where it left off, starting with this site. The first change is a good fit for a first post, because it's about series.

---

## Series were hard to follow

I write a lot of posts in series, but the site didn't show them well:

- Each part had a small box with "Part 5 of 6" and links to the parts just before and after. To see the whole series, you had to leave the post.
- The end of a post had a one-line link to the next part.
- The box only appeared once a series had two published posts. "How It All Started #1" is the first of six planned posts about learning to fly, but it wasn't in a series at all.

## What changed

- **A series box.** Every post in a series now starts with "Part 5 of 6", a progress bar and every part in order, with the one you're reading marked. On phones it starts folded, so it doesn't push the post down. On wide screens it moves to the column on the right, above "On this page".
- **A card for the next part.** The end of each post shows the next part's title and description, so you know what you're clicking.
- **Series on the home page.** Cards with progress bars now sit above the recent posts. The recent list shows only the newest part of each series, so it's no longer four Rebuilding posts in a row.
- **The [series page](/series/)** shows the same progress bars.

## Planned parts, in public

"How It All Started #1" is now part 1 of **From Fear to Flight**, my flying series, and the other five parts are listed by name: Ground School, First Flights, First Solo, Planning Real Flights and The Checkride.

They show as "coming", not as links, and the card at the end of part 1 says Ground School isn't out yet. You can see what's coming before it's written.

The planned parts live in one small file. When I publish a part with the same number, it takes the planned part's place by itself, and renaming or dropping one is a one-line change.

## A series without an end

Lab Notes is the first series with no last part, and a full progress bar would make it look finished. So an ongoing series shows "new every week" and a blinking cursor instead, and its newest post ends with a card saying the next one is on its way.

A weekly series also gets long. Once a series passes seven parts, the box at the top of a post shows only the parts around the one you're reading, with a link to all of them.

## Checking it

I checked it in headless Chrome on phone and desktop, in light and dark: part 1 of a planned series, a middle part, the last part, and the checkdomain series, whose part 2 is in Indonesian.

Turning "How It All Started #1" into part 1 didn't post anything to Bluesky or change anything on AT Protocol. The post is too old to be announced automatically, and the sync left its record alone.
